# MetalFX vs FSR 3.1 preset 4 + RCAS on the port's own frames (3.00x, 1146x480 -> 3440x1440)

Both arms read the SAME dumped frame: the port's scene colour, its depth and the resolved motion image (BB_DUMP_TRIGGER), and both write 3440x1440. The MetalFX output is quantised to 8 bits (the port's own upscaler output format) first, so neither arm is judged on a finer grid. Each row is the mean over the last 3 dumped frames with the crop fixed; +- is the spread across them. T(0.10-0.20) and T(0.20-0.40) are the ratio of the MetalFX radial spectrum to the FSR one in that band: 1.00 = the same energy at that scale, >1 = MetalFX keeps more.
